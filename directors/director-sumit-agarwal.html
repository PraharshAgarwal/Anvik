Sumit Agarwal

Director, Anvik Innovbiz Pvt. Ltd.
Agri-Tech Entrepreneur Business Development Farmer Connection Last-Mile Distribution Market Expansion Stakeholder Relations

Sumit Agarwal is a dynamic business leader with a distinguished track record of building and scaling agri-tech ventures from the ground up. Combining deep domain expertise in agriculture with sharp commercial acumen, Sumit has consistently translated market opportunities into sustainable, high-growth businesses.

What truly sets Sumit apart is his direct, on-the-ground experience with farming communities. He has spent years building trust-based relationships with farmers across rural India, understanding their economics, seasonal cycles, and input needs at a granular level. This deep farmer connect gives him a rare ability to design distribution models that work in practice, not just on paper.

He has built and managed last-mile distribution networks that bridge the gap between manufacturer and farmer, ensuring that quality products like Fermented Organic Manure reach the fields that need them most, at the right time and right price. At Anvik Innovbiz, Sumit drives commercial strategy and farmer outreach, making sure the company's mission translates into measurable impact on the ground.

His Role at Anvik

Connecting Products to Markets

As Director, Sumit is the operational and commercial engine behind Anvik Innovbiz. He manages the company's day-to-day operations, from on-ground execution and team coordination to go-to-market strategy for both Compressed Bio Gas (CBG) and Fermented Organic Manure (FOM). He identifies the right buyers, structures supply agreements, and forges partnerships that ensure sustained revenue and market presence.

His farmer-centric approach means that Anvik's products are not only commercially viable, but genuinely transformative for the farming communities they serve, improving soil health, reducing input costs, and supporting sustainable agriculture across Uttar Pradesh and beyond.
