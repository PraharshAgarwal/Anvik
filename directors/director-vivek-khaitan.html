Vivek Khaitan

Director — Anvik Innovbiz Pvt. Ltd.
Manufacturing Expert Chemicals & Pharma Renewable Energy Plant Management Quality & Compliance

Vivek Khaitan is a seasoned manufacturing and operations leader with a career spanning high-complexity industries including chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and renewable energy. His cross-industry experience gives him a uniquely rigorous approach to plant management, combining the precision and compliance standards of chemicals/pharma with the scale and sustainability demands of clean energy production.

Having worked across manufacturing environments where process control, safety, and quality are non-negotiable, Vivek brings disciplined operational frameworks to Anvik Innovbiz. His deep understanding of plant engineering, supply chain logistics, and anaerobic digestion technology has been instrumental in establishing and scaling the company's manufacturing capabilities at its Pilibhit facility.

As Director at Anvik Innovbiz, Vivek oversees end-to-end plant operations, from feedstock procurement and digestion processes to gas purification and manure enrichment, driving continuous improvement and championing environmentally responsible manufacturing at every stage.

His Role at Anvik

Driving Operational Excellence

Vivek leads all manufacturing and plant operations at Anvik Innovbiz's facility in Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh. He is responsible for ensuring that the anaerobic digestion process runs at peak efficiency, from managing diverse feedstocks like press mud, Napier grass, paddy straw, and cow dung, to delivering purified CBG and enriched FOM to end markets.

His operational philosophy centers on reliability, sustainability, and continuous improvement, ensuring that every tonne of agri-waste processed yields maximum value for Anvik and its stakeholders.
