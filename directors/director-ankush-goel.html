Ankush Goel

Director — Anvik Innovbiz Pvt. Ltd.
Strategic Technology Leader Trusted CXO Advisor Global Dealmaker B-School Speaker Clean Energy Strategy Investor Relations

Ankush Goel is a seasoned technology and business leader with a proven track record of advising CXOs across industries on high-stakes strategy, digital transformation, and cross-border deal structuring. He brings that same rigour to Anvik Innovbiz, a company operating at the intersection of clean energy, agriculture, and institutional B2B markets.

His Digital, Domain, and Delivery (3D) framework, built over years of closing complex, multi-party deals, directly applies to Anvik's challenge of scaling its Compressed Bio Gas (CBG) and Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) businesses through Oil Marketing Company agreements, government procurement channels like the SATAT scheme, and institutional buyer networks.

At Anvik Innovbiz, Ankush steers investor relations, strategic alliance formation, and long-term market positioning, ensuring the company is not just operationally sound, but commercially well-connected and investment-ready as India's biogas sector scales rapidly under national policy support.
