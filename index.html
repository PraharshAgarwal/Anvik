Our Process

From Agri Waste to Clean Energy

Our state-of-the-art anaerobic digestion technology transforms biodegradable material into valuable products.

1

Feedstock Collection

We collect press mud, Napier grass, paddy straw, poultry litter, cow dung and other biodegradable materials.

2

Anaerobic Digestion

Micro-organisms break down biodegradable material in the absence of oxygen, producing raw biogas.

3

Gas Purification

Raw biogas is purified and compressed into Renewable Natural Gas (CBG), ready for distribution.

4

FOM Enrichment

The digestate is enriched using advanced technology to produce potent Fermented Organic Manure for farmers.
