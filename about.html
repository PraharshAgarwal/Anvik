About Anvik Innovbiz

Pioneering sustainable energy solutions and organic agriculture through innovation and commitment to Mother Earth.

Our Story

Building a Greener Future

Anvik Innovbiz Private Limited was incorporated on 21st September 2022, with a vision to revolutionize India's renewable energy and organic farming landscape.

With our corporate office in New Delhi and manufacturing facilities in Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh, we operate our own Anaerobic Digestion plants which recycle carbon in agricultural waste to produce Renewable Natural Gas and Fermented Organic Manure.

Our team comprises professionals from leading educational institutes and corporates globally, bringing decades of combined experience in agri-based business and renewable energy.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To transform agricultural waste into clean energy and organic inputs, creating value for farmers, communities, and the environment through sustainable innovation.

Our Vision

To be India's leading producer of Compressed Bio Gas and Organic Manure, contributing to energy independence and chemical-free farming across the nation.

Our Values

Sustainability, innovation, integrity, and farmer empowerment form the core of everything we do, from feedstock sourcing to final product delivery.
